Enhancing Grasp Stability in Prosthetic Hands via Passive Finger Abduction/Adduction

A tendon-driven prosthetic hand with a fully passive abduction degree of freedom, using two opposing torsion springs per finger, dual tendon routing — grasp compliance for improving grasp stability with no extra motors or weight.

More Detail (Done in my 3rd year of undergrad)

Wireless IMU Teleoperation of a Robotic Arm

A wearable two-IMU controller that drives a 4-DOF robotic arm in real time over ESP-NOW — subtracting one accelerometer from the other to isolate wrist motion from whole-arm motion, with no kinematic model.

More Detail (Done in my 2nd year of undergrad)
